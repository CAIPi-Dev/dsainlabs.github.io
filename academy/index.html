Every project runs the same six‑step method.

The DSAIN Reliability Cycle is the spine of every session — the difference between using AI and using it well. It’s what makes this more than a prompting class.

Questions parents ask

The honest answers.

Isn’t this just more screen time?+
No. Sessions are supervised, project‑based, and built around judgment — students make things and then critique and verify them. The goal is capability and original thinking, not passive consumption.
What about privacy and my child’s data?+
Students never enter personal or family information into any tool, and we use fictional or public material in class. We collect only what we need to run the program, keep the project work rather than full chat histories, and follow clear retention and deletion rules.
Will this encourage cheating — or help prevent it?+
Prevent it. We teach the line between getting help and outsourcing, how to think originally, and how to disclose exactly what AI contributed. Academic‑integrity habits are part of every session.
My child is 12, or in Grade 7 — can they join?+
Yes, with a parent‑controlled or education‑appropriate account that we’ll confirm with you. Where no compliant option exists for a particular tool, we set the minimum at age 13 / Grade 8 — we’ll be clear with you before you enrol.
What does my teen actually walk away with?+
A real portfolio project, a personal AI Use Charter they authored, a completion certificate, and a clear next level to grow into — through the Creator levels and toward the Grade 10–12 Builder track.
